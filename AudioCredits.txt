The recordings were converted to mono MP3 and adjusted for in-game playback.
Duty Time uses the 1.00-2.05 second portion, with short edge fades.
The shart clip plays once on failure and is balanced approximately 3 dB
below the average level of the selectable clips. It is excluded from Random.
